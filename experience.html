Selected Research Projects & Tools

Cyber Security Research in Cyber-Physical Systems

Sponsored by Haldia Petrochemicals Ltd. and TCG Foundation

Smart-grid security: Established a hardware-in-the-loop smart-grid testbed integrating an OPAL-RT real-time simulator, National Instruments CompactRIO controller, phasor measurement units, a phasor data concentrator, a voltage/current power amplifier, a GPS clock, and an automation controller. The platform supported closed-loop, real-time experiments to analyze how cyberattacks affect power-system control and communication and to test countermeasures, including secure and privacy-preserving communication protocols, under realistic operating and timing conditions.

Next-Generation Secured Internet of Things (IoT)

Funded by the Department of Science and Technology, Government of India

Secure smart-meter design: Developed a low-cost smart meter on a Raspberry Pi 4 for integration with a smart-grid testbed. The prototype incorporated a spoofing-resistant, physically unclonable function-based authenticated key-exchange protocol. Environmental testing in a CME bench-top chamber assessed SRAM-PUF reliability and authenticated key-exchange performance under changing operating conditions.

Centre on Hardware Security

Data Security Council of India and Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Government of India

Assessment of programmable logic controllers: Constructed a closed-loop experimental setup using National Instruments CompactRIO as the programmable logic controller and OPAL-RT as the real-time plant simulator. The setup implemented representative smart-grid control functions and supported vulnerability analysis under multiple attack scenarios, including the physics-aware PLC rootkit described in the HARVEY paper at NDSS 2017. It also enabled real-time testing of countermeasures and analysis of the operational consequences of compromised controller behavior under realistic operating and timing conditions.

KAVACH: Key Analysis and Vulnerability Assessment for Cryptographic Hardware

Co-developed with PROC SHIELD Private Limited

Co-developed a GUI-based platform for side-channel and fault analysis of cryptographic hardware. KAVACH controls FPGAs and oscilloscopes, captures power and electromagnetic traces, and integrates ChipWhisperer Lite for trace acquisition and fault injection. It supports Correlation Power Analysis for AES-128 key recovery and clock-glitch experiments for Differential Fault Analysis. By integrating acquisition, analysis, and fault-injection controls in one interface, the tool supports repeatable hardware-security evaluation.
