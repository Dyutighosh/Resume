Soumyadyuti Ghosh

Postdoctoral Associate, Center for Cyber Security

New York University Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates

I work as a Postdoctoral Associate at the Center for Cyber Security, New York University Abu Dhabi, with Prof. Michail (Mihalis) Maniatakos, and am a member of the Modern Microprocessor Architectures Lab (MoMA Lab). My research focuses on privacy-preserving computation using Fully Homomorphic Encryption and Multi-Party Computation for efficient, privacy-preserving keyword search and coordinated-abuse detection in end-to-end encrypted messaging systems. I also investigate class prior-free positive-unlabeled learning, emphasizing rigorous validation of their accuracy, robustness, and practical utility on real-world datasets.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur in 2025 under the guidance of Prof. Debdeep Mukhopadhyay and Prof. Soumyajit Dey, conducting my doctoral research within the Secured Embedded Architecture Laboratory (SEAL) and the High Performance Real-time Computing Laboratory (HiPRC). During my doctoral studies, I was a Visiting Research Scholar at New York University Abu Dhabi in 2023. My Ph.D. research developed privacy-preserving techniques for smart grids using differential privacy, secure multi-party computation, and homomorphic encryption while balancing computational efficiency, numerical accuracy, and system stability for practical real-time deployment.

Before beginning my doctoral studies, I earned a B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering from Jadavpur University in 2016 and worked as a Senior Software Developer and Project Lead at Wipro Digital and its Innovations Lab from 2016 to 2019.
